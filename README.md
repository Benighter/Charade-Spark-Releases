# Charade Spark

![Charade Spark illustrated game cards](https://charade-spark.openbackend-manager.workers.dev/media/feature.png)

Hold the phone to your forehead. Let your friends give clues. Guess the word before the clock runs out.

Charade Spark is a charades party game for Android. Tilt down for correct and up to skip. Play casually with the room or compete in named team tournaments.

[Get Charade Spark on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.benighter.charadespark&referrer=utm_source%3Dgithub%26utm_medium%3Dreferral%26utm_campaign%3Dgame_night)

[Watch a real round and see the game](https://charade-spark.openbackend-manager.workers.dev/?campaign=github)

The Starter pack has 24 words and works offline as a guest. Create your own decks in any language and play them offline too.

One permanent purchase unlocks 55 illustrated decks, five difficulty levels and Team Challenge. The unlock costs R5 in South Africa, with local pricing elsewhere. Sign in with Google to restore your purchase and connect before starting a full-game round. No ads or subscriptions.
